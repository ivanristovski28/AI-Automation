Questions

Questions owners ask us

What if the AI makes mistakes?

We add human review steps and checks wherever accuracy matters, and we start with a small pilot so you can see it working on your own data first.

Will this replace my employees?

The aim is to remove repetitive work so people can spend time on things that need judgment, customers and growth.

Is our data safe?

We sign an NDA before we start, work with access controls, follow GDPR for EU clients, and can discuss EU-hosted or private deployment options.

Can't we just use ChatGPT for this?

It helps with one-off tasks. We build automations into your daily systems so they run reliably, securely and without anyone copying and pasting.

What if we are not sure what to automate?

That is what the automation audit is for. We map your processes and rank the opportunities, so you start with the one that pays back fastest.
