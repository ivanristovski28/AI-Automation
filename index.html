Who we help

Built for the way your business really works.

Judgment, not transcription.

Your team spends a large part of the day reading, sorting and re-typing data from documents. Our AI agents extract the key information from contracts and case files, structure it and store it in your system, with full traceability and human review at the critical points. Your lawyers get their time back for legal judgment. We start with a pilot on one document type you choose, such as NDAs or engagement letters, so you can measure the real savings before committing to anything.

Every unit, at a glance.

Running apartments means juggling cleanings, bookings, incidents and supplies, usually across spreadsheets and scattered messages. One of us has optimized work protocols and cut supply costs at an apartment company, so we know this business from the inside. We automate task assignment, team notifications and inventory control, so your staff prepare each unit faster and you see the status of everything at a glance.

Less typing, fewer errors.

Customs paperwork is meticulous, and one mistake costs money: re-exports, transit, transfers between bonded and regular warehouses. Ivan has managed that flow inside an ERP, and Alek builds the systems that speed it up. Our agents read the documents, check them for inconsistencies, pre-load the data into your ERP and flag exceptions for a person to review. Less typing, fewer errors, faster turnaround.

The report arrives every morning.

Most companies already have the data, but nobody has the time to turn it into decisions. We build automated pipelines that collect information from your sources, clean it and present it in dashboards with plain-language conclusions. We’ve worked with clients across very different sectors, and we train your team so the tool actually gets used. The report that takes days today arrives every morning without anyone lifting a finger.
